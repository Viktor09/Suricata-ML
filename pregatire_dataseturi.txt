VII. VRRP

- Acest caz are la baza 2 routere, unul master si unul de backup. Ideea e ca se genereaza niste gratuitous arps atunci cand un router pica si celalalt vine si ii ia locul si se poate confunda cu atacul principal. Acest scenariu a fost gandit in felul urmator: Se captureaza trafic normala pana la minutul 5, unde victimele fac niste cereri de DNS, apoi o sa pice routerul principal si o sa ii ia locul cel secundar, dupa care o sa isi revina masterul, iar apoi o sa se faca flapping pt 2 minute, urmata de stabilizarea finala.

#!/bin/bash

VM="Router1"
NET_NAME="virbr-labs"

echo "Starting VRRP Scenario"
SECONDS=0

echo "[Minute 0] Normal traffic starts now!"
sleep 300

echo "[Minute 10] Failover"
virsh destroy $VM
sleep 240

echo "[Minute 14] Failback"
virsh start $VM
echo "Waiting for Master to boot!"
sleep 240

CURRENT_IFACE=$(virsh domiflist $VM | grep "$NET_NAME" | awk '{print $1}')

echo "[Minute 18] Flapping"
END_TIME=$((SECONDS + 480))

while [ $SECONDS -lt $END_TIME ]; do
    echo "Link Down"
    virsh domif-setlink $VM $CURRENT_IFACE down
    sleep $((2 + RANDOM % 3))

    echo "Link Up"
    virsh domif-setlink $VM $CURRENT_IFACE up
    sleep $((6 + RANDOM % 5))
done

echo "[Minute 26] Stop Flapping"
virsh domif-setlink $VM $CURRENT_IFACE up
virsh start $VM 2>/dev/null
sleep 240

echo "[Minute 30] STOP!"
